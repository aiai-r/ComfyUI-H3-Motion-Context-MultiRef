# Update 4 — FL2VA Exact Song-Latent Masking

Update 4 adds an experimental MiniMax H3 workflow for song-driven video generation where the **original master song is placed directly into H3's target audio latent and fully protected from denoising**.

The goal is to keep one authoritative audio clock for the whole project. Reference images can still establish identity and appearance, but the song itself no longer needs to be supplied as `ref_audio_*` conditioning.

## New node

### H3 Song Audio + Masked Video Context

Class: `MiniMaxH3SongMaskedAVContext`

The node accepts a target MiniMax H3 joint AV latent, an H3 audio VAE, a master audio file, and a start time on the master timeline. It can also take a previous decoded video clip plus the video VAE for visual continuation.

For each raw clip it:

1. derives the target pixel-frame count from the H3 video latent,
2. verifies that the paired audio latent matches H3's 40 Hz audio clock,
3. extracts the exact master-audio interval beginning at `clip_start_seconds`,
4. resamples that interval to the audio VAE rate when necessary,
5. VAE-encodes it and writes it into the target H3 audio latent,
6. creates an audio denoise mask containing only `0`, so the audio stream is preserved,
7. optionally VAE-encodes the final `context_length` frames of the previous clip into the target video prefix,
8. sets only that video prefix to mask `0`, leaving the future video region at mask `1`.

The node returns the prepared joint latent, the actual preserved visual-frame count, and the exact audio slice used for that raw clip.

## FL2VA + reference images

The delivered example uses the FL2VA checkpoint and still supplies two image references through `MiniMaxH3ReferenceToVideo`. This is intentional: image references continue to occupy the normal reference-conditioning path, while the song occupies the target H3 audio latent.

All `ref_audio_*` sockets are disconnected in the example.

The successful experimental workflow retained some `<Audio 1>` wording inside its text prompts from the earlier reference-audio version. Those strings are kept because the example is meant to preserve the tested graph and prompts; they do **not** correspond to a connected `ref_audio_*` input in this workflow.

## Timeline math

The example uses 362 raw frames at 24 fps with 39 frames of visual continuation context.

```text
raw clip duration       = 362 / 24 = 15.083333 s
visual overlap/context  = 39 / 24  = 1.625000 s
new timeline advance    = 323 / 24 = 13.458333 s
```

Therefore consecutive master-song slices begin `13.458333 s` apart. The overlapping `1.625 s` at each seam is the same interval of the same original master song that was present when the preserved video prefix was generated.

## Final soundtrack

Per-clip H3 audio decodes are included only for inspection. The final delivered video uses the untouched full master song as its soundtrack.

The final enabled raw clip can extend beyond the end of the song. `MiniMaxH3SongMaskedAVContext` pads any missing tail with silence, while the final `VHS_VideoCombine` uses `trim_to_audio` so the delivered video ends with the master song.

The feature node itself uses the same H3/ComfyUI environment as the rest of this repository. The delivered workflow additionally uses:

- ComfyUI-KJNodes
- ComfyUI-VideoHelperSuite
- rgthree-comfy

The optional SolAttn/SageAttention patch nodes saved in the workflow are bypassed and are not required for the demonstrated path.
