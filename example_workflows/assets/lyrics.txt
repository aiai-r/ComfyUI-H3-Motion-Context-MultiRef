You were young in the doorway. I was tired in your coat. You asked if I was leaving. I said, I think I already wrote your name somewhere I can't find. And we were older by the ocean, laughing like it hurt. You reached for me so naturally, I forgot which life came first. And all I know is every time you look at me, I lose a year. You keep becoming someone new. I keep remembering you here. So if we meet before we meet, don't tell me who you are. Just stay until the room goes dark. I'll know you by the scar.
